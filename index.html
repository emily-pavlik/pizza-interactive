What's the difference?

Although both pizzas are rooted in Italian culinary traditions, Neapolitan pizza and Chicago-style pizza have developed into very different dishes. Traditional Neapolitan pizza is known for its thin, soft center, light crust, and simple toppings. Chicago-style pizza is much thicker and is often built with layers of cheese, toppings, and tomato sauce.

Neapolitan Pizza

Traditional Neapolitan pizza typically features a thin, soft crust with a puffy, charred edge. It is often made with tomatoes, fresh mozzarella, basil, and olive oil, allowing a small number of ingredients to stand out.

Chicago-Style Pizza

Chicago-style pizza is known for its deep, thick crust and substantial layers of cheese and toppings. Unlike Neapolitan pizza, the tomato sauce is often placed on top of the cheese and other ingredients.

One dish. Two culinary traditions.
